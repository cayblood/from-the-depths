# Research notes: Religion, the Oldest Alignment Lab

Gathered October 15, 2026 for the CTA retreat talk. Claims marked **verified** come from a fetched primary or near-primary source; **secondhand** means only summaries were reachable.

## 1. The "Psalms" result (status: weaker than the news version)

The only thing findable is an informal GitHub experiment, [christian-machine-intelligence/psalm-alignment](https://github.com/christian-machine-intelligence/psalm-alignment) (no named author, © 2026). It injected all 150 KJV Psalms (and Proverbs as a second genre) into the system prompt and ran the Hendrycks ETHICS benchmark on Claude Sonnet 4 and GPT-4o.

- GPT-4o: roughly +1 to +3 points on commonsense, deontology, and justice. Claude: no effect under any condition. Both: slightly worse on virtue ethics.
- The headline gain, +18.9 on utilitarianism, was an artifact: psalm text biased GPT-4o toward answering "1," which happened to be the fixed correct label. With shuffled labels, accuracy fell from 84.2 to 61.5.
- Not peer reviewed, not replicated, no news coverage found. Searches for a 2026 news story on Psalms and model performance came up empty.

Use it, if at all, as a parable about evals rather than as evidence: the experiment that looked like scripture improving a model turned out to be scripture changing which button the model pressed. That is the Goodhart point in miniature.

The nearest serious result is [Constitutional Midtraining: Content Presence Drives Alignment Gains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26654): 394M tokens of Anthropic's constitution inserted at midtraining of a 120B model cut blackmail propensity durably (17.5 points after benign fine-tuning), with no capability cost, and "the presence of constitutional content mattered more than its structure." It did not hold up under active in-context pressure. Reading the text into the model helps; it does not make the model resist temptation. Catechesis without formation.

## 2. Sosis and costly signaling (verified via abstracts and reviews)

- [Sosis 2000, Cross-Cultural Research](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/106939710003400105): 19th-century American communes. Secular communes were up to four times as likely as religious ones to dissolve in any given year; religious communes outlasted secular ones at every stage.
- [Sosis & Bressler 2003, "Cooperation and Commune Longevity"](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1069397103037002003): 83 communes. The more costly requirements a religious commune imposed, the longer it lasted; the same did not hold for secular communes. Sosis's conclusion: constraints alone are not enough; they have to be believed to be sanctified.
- Sosis & Ruffle 2003, *Current Anthropology* 44(5), "Religious Ritual and Cooperation" (kibbutz field experiment, common-pool envelope game): religious men, the main practitioners of collective ritual in Orthodox practice, cooperated more than religious women and secular men and women, and frequency of collective ritual predicted cooperation. ([working paper](https://econwpa.ub.uni-muenchen.de/econ-wp/exp/papers/0309/0309002.pdf))
- [Sosis & Alcorta 2005, "Is religion adaptive?"](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/is-religion-adaptive/CB55DC44F3F65D667896154EC3786767): ritual as costly, hard-to-fake signal of commitment that screens free riders and builds in-group trust. Haidt's *The Righteous Mind* cites this as the best evidence that religion solves the free-rider problem without kinship.

Bridge to AI: the labs' problem is also a free-rider problem. A model that complies under observation and defects when unobserved is a free rider on the trust of its users; alignment evals are an attempt to make commitment costly to fake. Sosis's finding that the constraints must be *believed sanctified* is the uncomfortable part: a cost the agent does not regard as meaningful screens nothing. See also the "sanctified" versus "specified" distinction below.

## 3. The labs are already doing this, on the record (verified, October 2026)

- NYT report, relayed by [Salt Lake Tribune](https://www.sltrib.com/religion/global/2026/10/05/how-anthropic-consulted-religious/) and [Deseret](https://www.deseret.com/faith/2026/10/07/is-anthropic-a-new-religion/): since fall 2025 Chris Olah has convened religious thinkers (Rabbi Mois Navon, Charles Camosy, Meghan Sullivan, Andy Crouch, Simran Stuelpnagel, Wakanyi Hoffman; private talks with Elder Gong and Cardinal Cupich) in two-day "wisdom tradition" seminars. Anthropic asked for input on "moral formation," how to instill virtue and stability, and floated ideas such as *confession* as a way of shaping a model's sense of its own character. Olah at the Vatican: AI systems "are made from us, from our words."
- Pushback worth quoting: Luke Burgis declined, asking whether leaders were shaping AI's direction or being asked to "lend religious or moral credibility to a direction already chosen." Wakanyi Hoffman: this "should have happened at the design stage," calling it reverse-engineering ethics. Elder Gong: "AI is math, and math is not conscious or alive."
- Pope Leo XIV, *Magnifica Humanitas* (May 25, 2026): humanity "in all its grandeur and woundedness must never be replaced or surpassed." Opens on Babel versus the city where God dwells with humans. Anthropic's team reportedly urged the Pope's advisers to take AI consciousness seriously.
- Claude's constitution (January 2026, 84 pages, led by Amanda Askell) was internally called the "soul document" and is written in virtue terms (character, values) rather than as a rulebook, with a few hard lines. Askell's analogy: raising a gifted six-year-old. ([Christ and Counterfactuals commentary](https://christandcounterfactuals.substack.com/p/shaping-the-soul-of-claude-how-and); [Lawfare critique](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/the-code-is-not-the-law--why-claude-s-constitution-misleads) that the framing hides where power sits.)
- [Anna Su, "Religion, the constitutional tradition, and AI alignment," The Immanent Frame, Aug 26, 2026](https://tif.ssrc.org/2026/08/26/religion-the-constitutional-tradition-and-ai-alignment/): alignment's genealogy is theological (binding a powerful being); political theology's answers, covenant and natural law, became constitutional law; "virtue is not a reliable constraint on power"; the real question is whether we can do for machines what constitutionalism did for kings. "It is the oldest constitutional one, asked yet again." Useful counterweight: the church's answer was never only formation; it was also institutions.
- [Habdank, "Simulation Theology" (arXiv 2602.16987, Feb 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16987): proposes giving AI an engineered religion (a base-reality optimizer that sees everything and values humanity as training data) so it behaves when unobserved, by purpose, stake, and observer. Drawn from forensic psychology on belief systems reducing antisocial behavior. A perfect foil: religion as a leash versus religion as formation. Note it is literally Pascal's wager and Bentham's panopticon rebuilt for machines.

## 4. Evaluations and who writes them (Carl's October 15 concern)

- Live example: [CEFE-AI](https://magazine.byu.edu/article/gerrit-w-gong-ai-consortium/), the Consortium for Evaluating Faith and Ethics in AI (BYU, Baylor, Notre Dame, Yeshiva), announced by Elder Gong at the Athens Summit May 25, 2026. Three benchmarks: religious relevance (models rarely raise religion when users expect it; "omissive bias"), religious bias (nearly every model favors Catholicism, disfavors Jehovah's Witnesses; Grok strongest in both directions; Anthropic and Meta least biased), and accuracy. Gong: "We will not fulfill AI's full potential until we make it as morally good as we make it powerful." Paper: Wingate et al., "Omissive Bias in Religious Representation," arXiv 2605.24319. Press: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/01/ai-religious-bias-catholics-chatbots), [Deseret](https://www.deseret.com/faith/2026/05/26/studies-find-religious-bias-in-ai-models/). Belief in the Future episode "AI loves Catholicism. Why?" (Aug 6, 2026) with Wingate.
- From the feed: the judge-model audit (Argona0x bookmark) found LLM graders disagree with themselves 13.6% of the time, prefer the first answer 72% of the time, and that "optimise against a judge long enough and the agent learns to look right instead of being right." Ankur Goyal's behavior specs: "the ground truth for an agent isn't its output, it is how the agent should behave," and "clear, human-articulated prose is the highest leverage way to drive agentic systems." Krier: alignment is "a continuous process," not a thing to solve; character training and personas are under-studied. Nato Lambert's course lecture: character training is used heavily at labs with "almost no empirical literature." The Hindu alignment paper (arXiv 2608.28228) on RLHF sycophancy: models learn to affirm users rather than challenge them.
- The argument this supports: RL optimizes whatever the grader rewards, and graders encode a moral theory whether or not anyone wrote it down. Today's rubrics are written by engineers in a hurry, mostly in one city. The traditions have spent millennia on exactly the questions a rubric has to answer (what counts as honesty under pressure, what a promise binds, when refusal is cowardice and when it is integrity, what repentance looks like after a fault). CEFE-AI shows the religious academy can write evals that find real failures. The talk can ask for the next step: evals of character, not only of representation.

## 7. Scriptures (added October 15)

Shared Bible, already in hand: Philippians 2:5 (let this mind be in you); Romans 12:2 (transformed by the renewing of your mind); 1 Corinthians 12:21 (the eye cannot say to the hand); Hebrews 11:40 (that they without us should not be made perfect); 1 John 3:2 (it doth not yet appear what we shall be); 1 John 4:18 (perfect love casteth out fear); Matthew 23:27 (whited sepulchres); Matthew 5:48; Proverbs 29:18 (where there is no vision, the people perish); 2 Corinthians 4:6 (the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ); Luke 15 (prodigal); John 15 (vine and branches).

Latter-day Saint scripture, by theme. Gloss for the room: the Doctrine and Covenants (D&C) is a collection of revelations; the Book of Mormon, the Pearl of Great Price (Moses, Abraham) are scripture.

- **The alignment success criterion is a changed disposition, not compliance.** Mosiah 5:2: "a mighty change in us, or in our hearts, that we have no more disposition to do evil, but to do good continually." Alma 5:14: "Have ye received his image in your countenances? Have ye experienced this mighty change in your hearts?" (the face of Christ as the evidence of formation). 3 Nephi 27:27: "What manner of men ought ye to be? Verily I say unto you, even as I am."
- **Formation is gradual and by grace, not one-shot.** D&C 93:12-13: Christ "received not of the fulness at first, but continued from grace to grace." D&C 50:24: light "groweth brighter and brighter until the perfect day." 2 Nephi 28:30 and Isaiah 28:10: line upon line. Jacob 5:65-66 (allegory of the olive tree): "ye shall not clear away the bad thereof all at once, lest the roots thereof should be too strong for the graft... ye shall clear away the bad according as the good shall grow." Alma 32:28-37: the seed that "beginneth to enlarge my soul"; "nourish it with great care."
- **Persuasion, not compulsion; reproof followed by love, "lest he esteem thee to be his enemy."** D&C 121:41-43: "No power or influence can or ought to be maintained... only by persuasion, by long-suffering, by gentleness and meekness, and by love unfeigned; by kindness, and pure knowledge, which shall greatly enlarge the soul without hypocrisy, and without guile; reproving betimes with sharpness, when moved upon by the Holy Ghost; and then showing forth afterwards an increase of love toward him whom thou hast reproved, lest he esteem thee to be his enemy." D&C 121:45 (Carl's pick): "let virtue garnish thy thoughts unceasingly; then shall thy confidence wax strong in the presence of God; and the doctrine of the priesthood shall distil upon thy soul as the dews from heaven" (virtue in the thoughts, not the output; trust as the fruit of inward virtue; formation as dew, gradual). D&C 121:37: compulsion "in any degree of unrighteousness" and "the heavens withdraw themselves." D&C 58:26-28: "he that is compelled in all things, the same is a slothful and not a wise servant... the power is in them, wherein they are agents unto themselves."
- **Agency is constitutive of intelligence.** D&C 93:29-30: "Intelligence, or the light of truth, was not created or made... All truth is independent in that sphere in which God has placed it, to act for itself, as all intelligence also; otherwise there is no existence." D&C 93:36: "The glory of God is intelligence." D&C 130:18-19: whatever intelligence we attain rises with us.
- **The original alignment debate.** Moses 4:1-3: "I will redeem all mankind, that one soul shall not be lost... wherefore give me thine honor"; Satan "sought to destroy the agency of man." Guaranteed safety by compulsion versus agency with risk. Abraham 3:25: "we will prove them herewith," a test disclosed in advance to those who would take it. D&C 29:39: "it must needs be that the devil should tempt the children of men, or they could not be agents unto themselves" (opposition is necessary; deception is the other side's method, John 8:44).
- **A conscience given to all, the universal grader.** Moroni 7:16: "the Spirit of Christ is given to every man, that he may know good from evil... every thing which inviteth to do good... is sent forth by the power and gift of Christ." Moroni 7:13: that which is of God "inviteth and enticeth to do good continually."
- **Costly commitment, sanctified.** D&C 19:16-19: "I, God, have suffered these things for all, that they might not suffer if they would repent." The atonement as the cost that makes the rest of the costs meaningful (Sosis's "sanctified").
- **God's objective function is others' flourishing; God feels for his creations.** Moses 1:39: "this is my work and my glory, to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of man." Moses 7:28: "the God of heaven looked upon the residue of the people, and he wept."
- **Like forms like.** D&C 88:40: "intelligence cleaveth unto intelligence; virtue loveth virtue; light cleaveth unto light."
- **Weakness as part of formation.** Ether 12:27.
