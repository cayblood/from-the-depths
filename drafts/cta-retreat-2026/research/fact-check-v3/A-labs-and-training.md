| 2 | Training stages: pretraining by next-word prediction on trillions of words; supervised fine-tuning on example answers; RL where many samples are graded and the model is updated toward high-scoring ones; the grade is the reward | **Confirmed** (with nuance) | Matches the canonical recipe in Nathan Lambert's *RLHF Book* (Manning, July 2026; arXiv 2504.12501): pretraining ends with "autoregressive pretraining"; post-training is "a many-stage process using three optimization methods": instruction/supervised fine-tuning ("teach formatting and form the base of instruction-following"), preference fine-tuning (RLHF, with "a reward model of human preferences" trained on collected comparisons), and RLVR, "the newest type of post-training that boosts performance on verifiable domains." The RL step: the policy generates completions, a "scalar sequence-level reward" scores each prompt-completion pair, and the optimizer raises the likelihood of completions with positive advantage. ## Newer things (2026) the author should know

- **The NYT report is a late-September story, and it was not the first.** The Washington Post broke the Christian summit on April 11, 2026 (about 15 Christian leaders, late March, two days at Anthropic HQ, Fr. Brendan McGuire and Brian Patrick Green named). Dias's NYT piece (Sept 29) widened the picture to the multifaith "wisdom tradition" seminars, the Gong and Cupich private talks, the Vatican lobbying, and the consciousness angle, and interviewed about 20 participants. Most commentary since (Reason Oct 2, Breitbart Oct 5, Deseret Oct 7) focuses on consciousness and NDAs, not moral formation. If the talk leans on "moral formation" and confession, those come from the NYT text, which is the right source.
- **Anthropic's outreach has expanded beyond religion.** Per the NYT, the company now also convenes psychologists and civil society leaders, and held another convening with religious thinkers in September 2026. The "wisdom tradition" circle is one strand of a broader program.
- **Pope Leo XIV's encyclical *Magnifica Humanitas* (May 25, 2026)** rejected AI consciousness after Anthropic's team had urged the Pope's advisers to take it seriously; the NYT says Olah considered withdrawing from a Vatican event afterward. This is the most consequential religious-institutional response to the labs so far and worth one sentence if the room includes Catholics.
- **Midtraining is now a named stage.** The Oxford paper (July 2026) and the work it cites (Tice et al. on pretraining-data alignment) make "midtraining" a standard term for the late-pretraining phase where labs mix in curated data. The lay description "placed it among everything else the model read, near the end of its reading" is safe.
- **OpenAI's Model Spec was revised August 18, 2026** (previous release Dec 18, 2025). Religion content did not change between the two; both have the same six brief mentions.
- **Sycophancy literature has a 2026 mechanism paper** (Shapira, Benade, Procaccia, Feb 2026) that formalizes why preference optimization amplifies agreement; useful if anyone in the Q&A asks "is sycophancy really caused by the grader?"
- **The Lutz and Weyl FAccT paper (June 2026)** is the citable published form of the faith-leader red-teaming work. Its headline claim is that faith leaders surface communal harms that individual-psychology framings miss. The snake-handling scorer anecdote is podcast-only as far as public text shows; re-listen to episode 29 at 28:52 before quoting precise wording, and do not attribute it to a Microsoft report.
- **The Microsoft 2026 Responsible AI Transparency Report (Sept 1, 2026)** describes an External Red Team Alliance with 18 universities, an AI Red Teaming Agent, and RAMPART (turning red-team findings into repeatable tests). If Lutz said on the podcast that the leaders' scenarios became repeatable tests, RAMPART is probably what she meant, but the report does not mention the faith-leader work.
